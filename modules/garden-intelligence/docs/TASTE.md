# Design and evidence profile

Audience: an older storyteller supported by a family member, and a gardener preserving practical experience. Outcome: capture, correct, privately save, then intentionally hand off one card.

Direction: warm paper, dark green text, readable serif headings, familiar form controls, large touch targets, no motion or artificial activity feed. Avoid scientific-certainty badges, token rewards, gamified pressure and generated testimonials. The leaf is a provisional product mark, not a change to approved Starlight brand identity.

References: RHS crop/context navigation; GrowVeg observation continuity; Seed Savers story/provenance relationship. Public page content inspected; signed-in product usability and causal business outcomes unverified. Our adaptation combines source wording, uncertainty and consent in one capture flow.

Hard gates: no fabricated saved state, no public-by-default storage, no invented testimony, no lost original account, no unreadable mobile layout, no export without permission.

Producer self-review: desktop screenshot and synthetic capture journey inspected; phone/tablet upper layouts inspected in browser iframe viewports. Independent review covers application behavior and security tests, not aesthetic scoring. Full accessibility and real-elder usability studies are pending.
