A simple family ritual.

  1. Send one interview prompt.
  2. Let them answer in their own ChatGPT, using voice or text as available.
  3. They correct the summary and choose what to share.
  4. They send the selected card to you. You save it with their permission.

Exported copies leave this notebook’s control. Deleting your copy cannot delete a relative’s copy.
