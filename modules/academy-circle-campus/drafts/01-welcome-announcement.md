## Your 48-hour arrival path

You do not need to binge ten hours of video to begin. Take three focused steps across your first 48 hours:

1. **Pick your primary builder lens (10 minutes):**
   - *Enterprise Systems Architect* — governance boundaries, ADRs, trust firewalls, and multi-provider resilience.
   - *Production AI Builder* — offline Python/TypeScript labs, hybrid retrieval, MCP tool servers, and golden evals.
   - *Technical GenCreator* — visual system diagrams, `design.md` brand architecture, and code-driven Remotion explainers.
   - *Structured Workflow Operator* — workflow triage (`automate` vs. `assist-only` vs. `do-not-automate`), human gates, and recovery playbooks.
2. **Try a 15-minute unaided framing pass (15 minutes):**
   Before asking an AI assistant for answers, pick one recurring workflow you care about and write down where an agent helps, where a human must approve, and what measurable signal (`killCriterion`) would tell you to shut the automation off.
3. **Post your first 5-line Field Note (15 minutes):**
   Head to the **Week 1 Field Notes** thread and share a public-safe snapshot of that workflow boundary. Once your first signal is in, you can opt into a 3–5 person Creation Cell or stay on the solo async track at your own pace.

## Three ground rules that protect everyone's work

- **Public-safe by default:** Share synthetic examples or redacted architecture patterns—never paste client secrets, API keys, or confidential employer records.

- **Helpful critique, zero vanity theater:** There are no login streaks or message-count leaderboards here. Standing comes from tested work and thoughtful 20-minute peer reviews.
