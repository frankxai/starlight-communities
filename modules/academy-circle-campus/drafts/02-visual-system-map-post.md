## The 12-week spine across three acts

Whether you move week by week with a 3–5 person Creation Cell or explore asynchronously, the curriculum builds across three four-week acts:

1. **Act I (Weeks 01–04) — Foundations & Firewalls:** Workflow triage (`automate` / `assist-only` / `do-not-automate`), hybrid RAG chunk geometry, `T0/T3` prompt-injection firewalls, and zero-trust MCP tool servers.
2. **Act II (Weeks 05–08) — Skills, Multi-Agent Loops & Evals:** Portable `SKILL.md` compilation, maker-checker state machines, memory privacy boundaries, and non-compensable golden evaluation gates.
3. **Act III (Weeks 09–12) — Multimodal Studio & Production Runbooks:** `design.md` visual systems, code-driven Remotion video explainers, provider gateway economics, and end-to-end disaster recovery defense.
