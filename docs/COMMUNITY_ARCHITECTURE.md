# Community Architecture & Taxonomy

This document maps out the specific platform structure for the 3-tier Starlight / GenCreator community ecosystem.

## Tier 1: Agentic Creator Community (Discord)
**Platform:** Discord
**Target:** Broad audience, builders, AI enthusiasts, GenCreator users.
**Vibe:** Fast, real-time, interactive, builder-centric.

### Category: ⛩️ The Gateway (Onboarding)
- `#start-here` (Read-only, rules, onboarding)
- `#introductions` (Where new members introduce themselves)
- `#announcements` (Broadcasts from Frank and key agents)

### Category: 🛠️ The Lab (Building & Tools)
- `#gencreator-studio` (Support and showcases for GenCreator)
- `#agentic-workflows` (Sharing n8n, LangChain, MCP workflows)
- `#prompt-engineering` (Sharing and optimizing prompts)

### Category: 🚀 The Arena (Quests & Proof)
- `#weekly-quests` (Automated posts from the Quest Designer Agent)
- `#visible-proof` (Where members drop links to what they shipped)
- `#leaderboard` (Managed by Accountability Agent)

### Category: 🗣️ The Commons (General)
- `#general-chat`
- `#wins-and-failures`
- `#collab-matching` (Agent-driven pairings)

---

## Tier 2: Starlight Founder Collective (Circle)
**Platform:** Circle (or equivalent structured community platform)
**Target:** Paid members, serious entrepreneurs building agentic businesses.
**Vibe:** Premium, structured, mastermind-focused, asynchronous depth.

### Space Group: 🏛️ The Great Hall
- `Announcements`
- `Member Directory`
- `Mastermind Matchmaking`

### Space Group: 🧭 Weekly Creation Cells
- `Cell 1 - Alpha` (Private group of 5)
- `Cell 2 - Beta` (Private group of 5)
- *Note: Handled dynamically by Circle Architect Agent.*

### Space Group: 📚 The Library (Resources)
- `System Architecture Patterns`
- `Agentic Income Playbooks`
- `Call Recordings & Transcripts`

### Space Group: 💎 The Boardroom (Discussions)
- `Business & Monetization`
- `Technical Deep Dives`
- `Hiring & Operations`

## Tier 3: Arcanea Syndicate (Stealth/Invite-Only)
**Platform:** Private Telegram Group / Bespoke Web Portal + High-touch Email.
**Target:** Dream 100, Billionaires, Top AI Lab Execs.
**Vibe:** Ultra-exclusive, low noise, extremely high signal.

### Structure
- **No public channels.**
- 1-on-1 concierge onboarding (managed by Frank + CRM Agent).
- Private quarterly strategy memos (delivered via secure link or PDF).
- `Syndicate Comms`: A single, highly curated, low-volume group chat (e.g., Signal or Telegram) for critical alpha and deal flow.
- Private VIP dinners/events coordination.
