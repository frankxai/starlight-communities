### WEEK 1: Visible Proof: Mind Map Quest (Founder Collective Edition)
**Theme**: Visible Proof
**Difficulty**: advanced
**Quest ID**: `week-1-visible-proof-mind_map`

#### Challenge Prompt
[Agentic Challenge] Create your model of human change and make the model visible. 
Requirement: Your approach must be agentic. Delegate at least one major component of this quest to an AI agent, script, or automated workflow to multiply your leverage.

Model, diagram, essay, or tldraw board. (Must include proof of the agentic workflow used).
