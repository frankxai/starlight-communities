# Community Brand Assets & Gen Prompts

To roll out the 3-tier community strategy, we need to generate visual assets using the `gen` skill across our established lanes (e.g., `noir-tech`, `cinematic`).

## 1. Agentic Creator Community (Tier 1)
**Vibe**: Dynamic, builder-focused, neon accents, accessible but high-tech.
**Lane**: `noir-tech` or `cyber-builder`

**Required Assets**:
- **Discord Server Banner**: "Generate a dynamic, wide-aspect server banner on the noir-tech lane showing interconnected glowing nodes representing builders and agents. Dark background with energetic cyan and purple accents."
- **Community Icon**: "Generate a sharp, minimalist icon representing an 'A' and 'C' interwoven with a digital spark. Noir-tech style, suitable for a small circle avatar."
- **Quest Completion Badges**: "Generate a set of 3 digital achievement badges (bronze, silver, gold) in a futuristic, sleek aesthetic for completing weekly agentic quests."

## 2. Starlight Founder Collective (Tier 2)
**Vibe**: Premium, structured, mastermind, deep knowledge, quiet luxury.
**Lane**: `cinematic` or `lux-tech`

**Required Assets**:
- **Circle Space Cover Images**: "Generate a series of 4 cinematic, ultra-premium wide banners (aspect ratio 3:1) for community spaces: The Great Hall, The Library, Weekly Cells, and The Boardroom. Use deep space imagery mixed with clean architectural lines, very subtle lighting, and deep contrast."
- **Member Avatar Frames**: "Generate a luxurious, subtle circular frame for profile pictures indicating 'Founding Member' status. Think brushed dark metal with a faint starlight glow."

## 3. Arcanea Syndicate (Tier 3)
**Vibe**: Exclusive, mysterious, institutional, billionaire-tier.
**Lane**: `monolith` or `dark-minimalist`

**Required Assets**:
- **Invitation Memo Header**: "Generate an ultra-minimalist, high-end letterhead graphic featuring the Arcanea emblem. Dark mode, using only subtle greys and a single pinpoint of pure white light."
- **Syndicate Access Card**: "Generate a photorealistic image of a heavy, matte-black metallic access card with subtle, encrypted runic patterns reflecting under soft studio lighting."
